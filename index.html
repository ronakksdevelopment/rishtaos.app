About RishtaOS

RishtaOS is a private relationship companion built for one job: helping you remember the stuff that actually matters — anniversaries, her birthday, the little things she mentioned once in passing.

It's not therapy, it's not a matchmaking app, and it definitely doesn't diagnose your relationship. Think of it as organisation with a bit of heart — an operating system for remembering the relationship stuff you really should remember.

Beta 1.0 · 100% local. Everything you enter is stored only on this device using your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent to any server.
